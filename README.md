I experiment with AI systems and the ways people interact with them — through voice, documents, terminals, and gestures.

## On the workbench: ENTITY

A voice-driven agent with a visual presence. I’m exploring an assistant that listens, works through tasks, and answers in character — with its changing state expressed through its presence as well as its voice.

**Private · Work in progress**

## Selected projects

- **[Meridian](https://github.com/R-Hruthvik/meridian-llmops)** — Document question-answering with hybrid retrieval, citations, and tools for inspecting retrieved sources.
- **[Fine Print Fighter](https://github.com/R-Hruthvik/fine-print-fighter)** — A consumer-dispute research tool for examining objections, checking source passages, and drafting cited responses.
- **[LAZYTYPE](https://github.com/R-Hruthvik/LAZYTYPE)** — Hotkey speech-to-text for Linux, with multiple transcription providers and a local fallback option.
- **[MusicCLI](https://github.com/R-Hruthvik/MusicCLI)** — A terminal music player and discovery tool with taste profiling and MCP integration.
